Remote Job Eligibility Checklist

A free checklist for developers applying to remote roles. Check these six points before you spend time on an application. The same checklist ships inside the Remote Developer Application Kit as a 50-row worksheet.

The six checks

  1. Country eligibility. Many remote roles are restricted to specific countries for tax or legal reasons. Look for a stated location restriction in the listing before anything else.
  2. Working hours. Check whether the role expects overlap with a specific time zone. "Remote" does not always mean "any time zone".
  3. Contract type. Note whether the role is employment, contractor or agency, and whether that works for your situation.
  4. Core skills. List the three to five core requirements. If you cannot point to real evidence for most of them, the application is likely a poor use of your time.
  5. Application channel. Follow the employer's stated process exactly. Do not invent a faster route.
  6. Next action with a date. Every application in progress should have one specific, dated next step. Undated intentions do not get done.

Sample question to send

When a listing does not state a restriction clearly, ask early, politely and briefly:

Hello, thank you for posting this role. Could you confirm whether the position is open to candidates based in [your country], and whether any working-hours overlap is expected? I want to make sure I am eligible before investing your time in the process.

Replace the bracketed placeholder, keep it to one short message, and send it through the employer's official channel.
